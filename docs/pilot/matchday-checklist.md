## ☀️ Before the first game

- [ ] Venue wifi tested at the control desk — hotspot phone charged and ready as backup
- [ ] Signed in to the admin console; **Match Entry** open for today
- [ ] Public results page open on a spare phone (your live sanity check)
- [ ] Scorecards handed out to courts / stewards
- [ ] Printed schedule pinned up
- [ ] Everyone entering scores knows their job (console or QR scan)

## 🏐 During play

- [ ] Enter each score as the game ends — Match Entry or QR capture
- [ ] Photograph completed paper scoresheets and attach them to the match
- [ ] Between rounds, check the status strip: every played match shows **Results in**
- [ ] Late team or no-show? Use the score form's **Late arrivals & forfeits** section — never type a fake score (default rule: 2 goals deducted per minute late; 4+ min or no-show = 10-0 forfeit)
- [ ] Court or time change? Unlock the schedule (🔒 toggle), move the fixture, check for clash warnings, re-lock
- [ ] **End of each phase** (e.g. end of pools): take a **Snapshot**, then on the division's **Format** page use **Resolve qualifiers → Apply resolved slots** to carry winners into the next stage

## ⚠️ If something goes wrong

| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| Wrong score saved | Open the match, correct it, save — everything recalculates |
| Internet drops | Keep playing on paper; enter the backlog when it returns |
| Signed out | Normal after 10 min idle — just sign back in, nothing is lost |
| Public page "not updating" | Check the score was saved, then refresh the page |
| Big mistake / disputed result | **Stop. Call support.** A snapshot restore can rewind the division |

## 🌙 End of day

- [ ] Every played match entered — nothing half-saved
- [ ] Public standings match the paper records
- [ ] **Snapshot** each division: `End of day <date>`
- [ ] Collect (or photograph) all paper scorecards
