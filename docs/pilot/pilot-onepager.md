Hello `<Club / league name>` 👋 — here's exactly what you get for your tournament on `<Tournament date>`, what we'll do for you, and what isn't part of this first run. No jargon, no surprises.

---

## What Tournamate does for your event

Tournamate is an **online tournament manager**. We help you set everything up, then on the day you (or your court helpers) enter scores and everyone sees live results on a public web page — including a free mobile app for spectators.

**You'll be able to:**

- 🏆 Run your tournament structure — divisions (age groups/streams), pools, round-robins, and knockout brackets.
- 👥 Add your teams and players (or send us a spreadsheet and we'll load them).
- 🗓️ See a clear schedule of which game is on which court and when.
- ✍️ Enter scores easily — on a laptop, tablet, or phone.
- 📲 Let court helpers submit scores by scanning a **QR code** — no logins to remember.
- 📸 Snap a photo of the paper scoresheet and attach it to the match, just in case.
- 📊 Show **live standings and results** on a public page anyone can open on their phone.
- 🧮 Use netball scoring out of the box (or tweak the points and tie-breakers to your rules).
- 🧑‍⚖️ Keep track of umpires and their assignments.
- 🖨️ Print scorecards and schedules for the courts.

## What's **not** included this time (and why that's OK)

This first pilot is deliberately focused so it's reliable and stress-free. Not included yet:

- **Working offline** — Tournamate needs internet at the venue. We'll help you plan wifi or a phone hotspot, and you keep paper scoresheets as a backup. *(Live online results are the whole point.)*
- **A downloadable admin app** — you just use a web browser on a laptop or tablet; nothing to install.
- **Online payments or ticketing** — handle team fees and entry however your club already does. Tournamate won't take payments.
- **A subscription to set up yourself** — there's nothing to buy in-app; we onboard you directly.
- **Your own web address / full branding** — your results page lives on the standard Tournamate link for now.
- **Push notifications / email alerts** — spectators just refresh the live page to see the latest scores.

Anything on this list that you'd love to have? Tell us — it helps shape what we build next.
